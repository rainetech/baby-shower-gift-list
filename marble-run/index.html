      Build a marble run that plays music

      Every piece of metal rings like a piano key when a marble lands on it.

      1. 1Add metalDrag pieces from the tray onto the pegboard.Tap a piece in the tray, then tap the pegboard. Each new piece plays the next note.
      2. 2Follow the dotsThe line shows where a marble will roll; each ring is a beat. Drag a bar near a ring and it snaps on, ringing right on the beat.
      3. 3Press PlayDroppers release marbles and your run plays its tune. Open Demos for songs made of marbles!
