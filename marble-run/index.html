How to play

Get the marbles from the tube into the cup!

  1. 1Drop a marble Click the cardboard tube at the top, or press Space.Tap the cardboard tube at the top, or the Drop Marble button.
  2. 2Slide the board Move your mouse left and right (or use ← →) to steer the board.Drag left and right anywhere to slide the board.
  3. 3Catch it in the cup Line up the board's exit with the cup — the rim glows green. Marbles that miss the cup are lost!
